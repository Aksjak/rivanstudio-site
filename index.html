Questions

Short answers.

What does Rivan Studio make?

Short vertical video ads for social media, starring our own fictional AI characters: Elise (home), Orin (comedy), Ada (morning routines) and Vera (summer and swim). You send product photos, and we make the ad.

What does a video cost?

A pilot is NOK 990 for one 10–20 second video, and three videos cost NOK 2,490 (NOK 1,900 on your first order). Outside Norway it's EUR 89 and EUR 229. No VAT is added while Rivan Studio is not VAT registered. We invoice after delivery with 14 days to pay.

How fast do we get it?

You get a one-line idea and the first frame the same day. The finished video arrives within 48 hours after you approve the idea, with one round of changes included.

Is the video labelled as AI?

Yes. Every video is labelled "AI-generated", and the characters are fictional.

Does the character endorse our product?

No. We never say a character has used, tested or recommends your product from experience. The video shows your product, and the product claims stay yours.

Where and for how long can we use it?

In your organic and paid social media for 3 months, for example Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Longer use is available on request. You may cut and subtitle the video; TV, outdoor and resale aren't included.
